# FAQ

This document answers **frequently asked questions** about AUCTORISEAL.
It is defensive by design. If a question is not answered here, it is either
out of scope or intentionally excluded.

---

## What is AUCTORISEAL?

AUCTORISEAL is an **authority and legitimacy system**.

It records **who is allowed to authorize actions**, under what scope and constraints,
and makes that authority verifiable, revocable, and replayable.

---

## Is AUCTORISEAL an AI system?

No.

AUCTORISEAL does not generate, decide, predict, or act.
It only records and validates **authority**.

---

## Is AUCTORISEAL a regulator or standards body?

No.

AUCTORISEAL is a **private authority system**.
It has no legal or regulatory power.
Its authority applies only within systems that explicitly defer to it.

---

## Does a seal mean an action is correct or safe?

No.

A seal means only that:
- someone was allowed to authorize the action
- within a defined scope and constraints
- at a specific time

Correctness, safety, and outcomes are outside scope.

---

## Why is this needed? Git already tracks commits.

Git tracks **who committed code**, not **who authorized AI to act**.

AI introduces power without personhood.
AUCTORISEAL exists to make authority explicit *before* action occurs.

---

## Can authority be revoked?

Yes.

Revocation is:
- explicit
- immediate
- recorded
- final

A revoked seal is never valid again.

---

## What happens if AUCTORISEAL is unavailable?

- Existing seals remain valid
- No new authority can be issued
- Consumers must degrade safely or fail closed

AUCTORISEAL prefers **loss of power over ambiguity**.

---

## Does AUCTORISEAL guarantee compliance?

No.

AUCTORISEAL provides records and determinism.
Compliance depends on how those records are used.

---

## Who operates AUCTORISEAL?

An individual or legal entity acting as the **operator**.

Operators do not gain authority by default.
Authority must still be explicitly issued and recorded.

---

## Is this overkill?

For casual tooling, yes.

For systems where:
- AI can mutate state
- mistakes are costly
- audits matter

it is the minimum viable control.

---

## Can this be forked?

Yes.

Forking copies code, not legitimacy.
Authority exists only where seals are issued and deferred to.

---

## Why is everything so strict?

Because authority without strictness becomes narrative.

AUCTORISEAL is designed to end arguments, not create them.

---

## Final Answer

> **AUCTORISEAL exists so that when something happens,  
> no one has to ask who allowed it.**

That is its only job.
